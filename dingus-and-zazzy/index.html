Case 01

Masterclass → Bootcamp launch

A full launch I strategized, wrote, designed and ran myself. Every channel said the same thing on the same day. $10K USD in one window.

StrategyPricingSocialEmailMeta AdsLanding pages

The problem

My previous launch: $1,000 USD × 6 students. Great ticket, tiny volume, and every seat came with hours of 1:1 calls I couldn't scale.

My point of view

Lower the entry price, unbundle the 1:1 as an upsell, and set the gap between tiers at $200 — less than my standalone $250 consult. The VIP tier becomes the obvious choice instead of the expensive one.

The result

$10,000 USD from a single launch — up from $6,000 the time before — with the 1:1 calls contained to the VIP tier instead of every seat.

What I built

  • Audience poll → 15 carousels attacking the two biggest objections
  • Masterclass landing, lead magnet, WhatsApp group, sales page with two tiers
  • Meta Ads traffic campaign + 3 reminder emails
  • 5 sales emails mirrored by 5 carousels: same argument, same day, email + feed

Warm-up content · blue = free

Sales sequence · gold = paid · mirrors the 5 emails

Content is in Spanish — my audience is LatAm. Happy to walk through any piece in English.

The analytics catch: mid-launch, Meta reported 478 "Leads" in a week against 225 real people. The pixel's Lead event was firing on a button click. Left alone, the algorithm would keep buying clickers, not registrants — so I flagged it and remapped the event before scaling spend.
