    FAQ

    Good questions

    Is the report really free?

    Yes. It's a free PDF. We'll send it to your inbox, along with a few short emails on how to use it. You can unsubscribe anytime.

    Where does the data come from?

    From a RankBreeze data study of 16,915 amenity additions across 7,434 active Airbnb listings. 5,992 of those listings had revenue data. For each one, we compared median daily revenue in the 90 days before vs. the 90 days after the amenity was added.

    Is this a guarantee?

    No, and we want to be upfront about it. This is observational before/after data, not a controlled experiment. Other things can change in those 90 days too, like the season, your prices or your reviews. Think of these numbers as strong hints about what's worth trying, not a promise of what you'll earn.

    Do I need a credit card for the trial?

    No. The RankBreeze trial is free for 14 days and doesn't ask for a credit card.
