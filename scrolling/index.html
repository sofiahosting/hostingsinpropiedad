How I'd think about doubling the community while keeping the quality bar. Ideas to align on, not fixed targets.

Qualified creators, sourced daily and kept active with a reason to stay.

Today · ~1,000Goal · 2,000

Bring them in

Daily outreach, plus channels that bring creators to us.

  • 🔎Search by nicheTikTok / IG search for gaming, apps, kids & wellness creators already making ads.
  • 🤝Referrals from Tier AYour best creators know other good creators. Reward the intro.
  • 🎙️Open webinar as the front doorA free monthly class for UGC creators → application form.
  • 📝One scored application formPortfolio, niche, devices, rates. Filter before the first call.

Keep them active

The best creators stay where they grow and get paid on time.

  • 📅Monthly creator webinarHook teardowns, filming tips, Q&A. Recordings in a library.
  • 📈Share results backCreators who see how their ads did make better ads.
  • ⭐Tiers with perksTier A gets first pick of briefs and a spotlight.
  • 💸Pay on time, alwaysThe simplest retention tool there is.
07 · First 90 days

Listen. Standardize. Scale.

How I'd approach the first three months. A starting point to align with you, not a fixed plan.

Days 1–30

Listen

"What does a great Scrolling creator look like?"

  • Learn your brief, standards and current roster.
  • Talk to top creators and the creative team.
Days 31–60

Standardize

"How do we make piece #100 easier than #1?"

  • One roster sheet, one feedback format, one naming rule.
  • Launch the first creator webinar.
Days 61–90

Scale

"Which profiles win, and how do we find more?"

  • Monthly performance read by niche × format × hook.
  • Referral loop and scored application form live.
