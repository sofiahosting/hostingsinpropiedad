01

What I did

  • Built the brand, e-commerce site and TikTok Shop from scratch.
  • Run TikTok Shop daily in Seller Center and Affiliate Center: creator outreach, free samples, commission plans, GMV Max campaigns and shop promotions.
  • Recruited and manage 50+ creators and affiliates, coordinated through Discord and WhatsApp communities.
  • Run paid social, email and organic content with weekly GMV and KPI tracking, and documented processes so the business runs on systems.

Results

50+creators & affiliates
What it shows: I know how to grow a DTC brand through creators and social commerce, and how to run it day to day.

Case 04 · Operations & market data

Operating 30+ short-term rental listings

I ran a short-term rental business in Guadalajara without owning the properties. Every decision on pricing, listings and guest experience came from data and guest feedback, and I turned that know-how into a product.

04

What I did

  • Operated 30+ Airbnb listings: owner relationships, setup, pricing and availability, and the cleaning and maintenance teams.
  • Ran ongoing market and competitor research on pricing, demand and listing performance.
  • Built AuditBnB, a 9-category listing audit (photos, title, reviews, pricing, calendar and more) that produces a 10-page report with prioritized recommendations.

Results

30+listings operated

10 ppreport per listing
