Rules my AI already knows before it starts

I never re-explain a brand. When a client corrects something once, I save it as a rule, and every future task follows it automatically.

Client corrects once Saved as a rule Never repeated
  • Brand colorsEach brand has a fixed palette. AI never improvises one.
  • Write for everyoneOver half the audience is men, so no copy written only for women.
  • Fonts the client rejectedWe tested Impact. The client hated it. It never comes back.
  • Ad copy ruleSell the result the customer gets, not the tool behind it.
  • Keep records in syncNew contract? Update the tracker and the calendar too.

Catching risk early

Diagnose first. Then escalate with evidence and a fix.

  1. Early test

    An ad was getting very cheap clicks.

  2. Diagnosed

    The hook promised more than the service could deliver.

  3. Flagged, with a fix

    Raised it before scaling the budget, with an honest rewrite ready.

  4. Result

    Higher cost per click. Better-fit leads.

“Cheap clicks that turn into angry calls aren't cheap.”
