Process

How I build a winning ad.

A simple loop, run every week.

1

One angle per video

One reason to buy per ad, so the data tells you which reason works.

2

Three hook variations per angle

Same body, three openings. The first 3 seconds are the test.

3

Launch and read in 48–72h

Hook rate and CTR first, then cost per lead.

4

Kill losers, iterate winners

Cut what doesn't hold attention. New hooks on the angle that does.

1 angle→3 hooks→48–72h→Kill / iterate↻
